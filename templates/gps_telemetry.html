RF Jammer vs. Cellular Shadow Detection Rule

How SmartMineGuard algorithmically distinguishes between legitimate rural cellular dead-zones and deliberate illegal mining jammer devices:

1. Legitimate Network Shadow NON-FRAUD
  • C/N0 Ratio: 42 - 48 dB-Hz (Normal RF floor)
  • GNSS Lock: 8 - 14 satellites visible
  • Cellular: Gradual ping latency & tower handover delay
  • Verdict: Terrain shadow (No violation alert)
2. Deliberate GPS Jammer Device ILLEGAL
  • C/N0 Ratio: Spikes to < 18 dB-Hz (RF saturation)
  • GNSS Lock: Instant collapse to 0 satellites (<3s)
  • Cellular: GSM modem transmits packets with 0 GNSS fix
  • Verdict: Illegal RF Jamming device active
3. Physical Wire-Cut / Tamper HARDWARE
  • Main Power: 24.2V drops instantly to 0.0V
  • Battery: Unit auto-switches to 3.7V internal LiPo
  • Sensor: Enclosure optical switch breached
