6 Core Strategic Pillars of Mineral Administration & Compliance

6 Core Pillars of Mineral Movement Administration — Engineered for the Directorate of Mines & Geology to ensure transparency, protect state royalty revenues, and enforce the Mines & Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957.

MMDR Act (1957) Compliance
01 Sec 21 MMDR

Single-Use e-Rawaana & Destination Verification

Eliminates duplicate trips on a single pass. Electronic transit passes automatically transition to CONSUMED upon buyer geofence arrival, preventing the re-use of valid paper passes for unrecorded hauls.

Destination Geofence Verify in Portal →
02 RTO Baseline

Automated Weighbridge Deck & Tare Audit

Optical infrared beams ensure truck axles are 100% on the scale platform. Empty tare weights are cross-audited against registered RTO unladen tare baselines (11.5 MT ± 8%) to prevent false tare inflation.

IR Position Sensors Weighbridge Desk →
03 Royalty Schedule

Mineral Grade & Royalty Classification

Cross-checks declared mineral quality against pit concession certifications and physical bulk density, preventing higher-grade mineral (Neela Maal @ ₹375/MT) from being billed as cheaper masonry gravel (Laal Maal @ ₹300/MT).

Density Audit Permits Ledger →
04 Mass-Balance

Processing Plant Inward Reconciliation

Audits raw mineral feedstock delivered into commercial stone crushers against valid electronic gate passes, uncovering unrecorded cash/token hauls and ensuring 100% statutory royalty collection on processed aggregate.

Crusher Facility Ledger Crusher Analytics →
05 EC Sanctioned

Sanctioned Environmental Quota Control

Compares 3D drone volumetric quarry elevation models (DEM) with cumulative e-Rawaana tonnage. Automatically suspends pass generation the moment dispatches reach 100% of sanctioned environmental clearance limits.

Volumetric DEM Sentry Dispatch Limits →
06 ISTP / AIS-140

Inter-State Corridor & Pit Dwell Sentry

Monitors designated inter-state transit corridors (e.g. NH-48) for valid Inter-State Transit Passes (ISTP) and tracks pit turnaround times to detect unrecorded loading or unauthorized vehicle delays beyond 90 minutes.

About SmartMineGuard

Platform overview and architecture supporting transparent mineral transport accounting

Quarry Pit & Haulage Loading Operations
Connecting the Mineral Transport Lifecycle Across the State

SmartMineGuard is an integrated digital monitoring platform developed to support state mining departments and leaseholders in maintaining rigorous compliance with the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act.

Lease Quota → e-Rawaana Pass → Weighbridge Ticket → Corridor Transit → Public Verification

By uniting transit permits, vehicle registrations, electronic weighment tickets, and corridor geofences into a unified ledger, the system provides transparent reconciliation between opening quarry stock, extraction volumes, and final dispatch.

Strict Mass-Balance Formula Direct arithmetic verification: Opening Stock + Today's Production - Today's Dispatch = Available Stock.
Role-Based Security Model Complete isolation between public statutory lookups and secured law enforcement command centers.
