⚖️ Administrative Operating Mechanism: How Treasury Enforces Deductions

1. Concrete Dimensions from e-MB: Junior Engineer enters physical dimensions (e.g. 4,500 m³). No contractor can tamper with this figure because it determines their overall road payment.
2. Automatic Sand Formula: By Indian Road Congress (IRC:15) mix-design formulas, 4,500 m³ strictly requires 2,025 MT of sand. The software computes this requirement automatically.
3. Automatic Cheque Deduction: If the contractor only shows passes for 1,600 MT, the system detects a 425 MT deficit and cuts ₹2,55,000 directly from their Running Account bill cheque before bank release.
